# Fox River Recreation: Owner's Guide

This guide shows how to update foxriverrecreation.com yourself. You don't need to write code or call a developer for routine changes such as rates, photos, the drone video, rules, or contact details.

Developer setup, deployment, and technical notes are in [DEVELOPMENT.md](DEVELOPMENT.md).

## 1. Sign in

1. Go to **https://foxriverrecreation.com/admin**. There is also an **Owner sign-in** link at the bottom of every page.
2. Click **Continue with Google** and choose your Google account.
3. The editor opens with the heading **Welcome back.**

Only approved Google accounts can edit. If you see "This Google account isn't approved to edit the site", you signed in with a different account, or the account hasn't been added yet. See [section 9](#9-give-or-remove-editing-access).

When you're done, click **Sign out** at the top of the editor.

## 2. How editing works: save, preview, publish

The editor has seven tabs: **Homepage**, **Rates**, **Photos**, **Amenities**, **Reviews**, **Rules**, and **Contact**. Each change goes through three steps.

1. **Edit.** Change any text, price, or photo. A red dot and **Unsaved changes** show that you have edits that aren't saved yet.
2. **Save draft.** Click **Save draft**. Your changes are stored privately, and visitors still see the old version. You can close the browser and come back later.
3. **Preview.** Click **Preview** to see the homepage and the rates, gallery, and rules pages exactly as visitors will. Click **Back to editing** to return.
4. **Publish.** Click **Publish changes**, then confirm. Visitors see the new version right away. Refresh the public site to check it.

**Publish changes** stays greyed out until your latest edits are saved. Save first, then publish.

The line under the buttons shows **Draft version** and **Published version**. When the two numbers match, the live site matches your draft.

## 3. Change rates

1. Open the **Rates** tab. Rates are grouped the same way as on the website: RV camping, seasonal sites, cabin rentals, on the water, passes & visitors, and gatherings & extras.
2. For each rate you can change:
   - **Rate name**, for example "Weekly campsite".
   - **Price in dollars**. Type numbers only, without the $ sign. Leave it blank to show "Please call".
   - **Per night / week / season**, the text after the price, for example "/ night".
   - **Additional details**, a short note under the price, for example "Plus electric".
3. Each group also has an introduction paragraph you can edit.
4. Save, preview, and publish.

A price changed here updates everywhere it appears, including the homepage cards and the rates page.

You can't add or remove rate lines in the editor. To add a new kind of rate, contact your developer.

## 4. Add, swap, or remove photos

Open the **Photos** tab. It has four areas: **Homepage hero** (the large photo at the top of the homepage), **Stay photos** (RV, cabins, seasonal), **River photo**, and **Photo gallery**. Every gallery photo appears on the Gallery page, and the first eight also appear on the homepage.

**Before you upload**

- Use JPG, PNG, WebP, or AVIF files up to 10 MB. Photos straight from a phone are fine.
- Wide (landscape) photos at least 1600 pixels across look best. The site resizes and compresses them for visitors automatically.
- Uploaded photos can be viewed by anyone who has the exact web address, even before you publish. Only upload photos meant for the website.

**Swap a photo**

1. Under the photo you want to change, click **Upload replacement** and choose the file.
2. Fill in **Photo description**, a short sentence about what the photo shows, for example "Kids playing in the pool on a summer afternoon". Screen readers read this aloud for blind visitors, and Google uses it too.
3. Save, preview, and publish.

**Add a gallery photo**

1. Scroll to the bottom of **Photo gallery** and click **Add a gallery photo**.
2. Write a photo description for the new photo.
3. The gallery holds up to 24 photos. Remove one to make room for more.

**Reorder or remove gallery photos**

- **Move earlier** moves a photo one spot toward the start of the gallery. The first eight are the ones on the homepage, so put your best photos there.
- **Remove from gallery** takes a photo off the website. The gallery must keep at least one photo.

## 5. Change the drone video

The drone video plays silently behind the headline at the top of the homepage. The hero photo shows while the video loads, and it's all that shows for visitors who have turned off motion on their device.

1. Open the **Photos** tab and find **Drone video** under **Homepage hero**.
2. Click **Replace drone video** (or **Upload drone video** if there isn't one) and choose the file.
3. Use an MP4 or WebM file under 50 MB. Shorter and smaller loads faster: 20 to 60 seconds and under 15 MB is ideal. The free [HandBrake](https://handbrake.fr) app can shrink a video with its "Web" presets.
4. The video plays without sound, so don't rely on audio.
5. To stop showing a video, click **Use photo only**.
6. Save, preview, and publish.

## 6. Post events

The homepage has a "There's always a reason to get together" section that sends visitors to the Fox River Recreation Facebook page for events and announcements. To announce an event, post it on Facebook. You don't need to change the website.

The park rules also tell guests to ask at the store about special activities and events. You can change that wording in the **Rules** tab.

## 7. Edit amenities, reviews, and park rules

**Amenities tab.** Change the name and short description of each amenity shown on the homepage.

**Reviews tab.** Change the guest quotes shown on the homepage. Keep each guest's wording and name accurate. Below the quotes, a **Read our reviews on Google** button sends visitors to your Google reviews. You don't need to update it.

**Rules tab.**

- Edit any rule's **Rule heading** and **Policy wording**.
- Click **Add a rule section** to add a rule, or **Remove section** to delete one.
- Tick **The complete park rules are included below and have been checked** once every rule is final. Until it's ticked, the website asks guests to contact the office for the full policy.

## 8. Update contact details and links

Open the **Contact** tab.

- **Office phone.** Use the 847-395-6090 format. Every call button on the site updates automatically.
- **Contact email, street address, city, state, and ZIP.** Keep the address exactly the same as on your Google Business Profile, including punctuation. Small differences make it harder for Google to tell they're the same business.
- **Camping season** and **Languages spoken.**
- **Facebook page link.** Leave it blank to hide all Facebook links on the website.
- **Google Business Profile link.** On Google, open your business listing, click **Share**, copy the link, and paste it here. The **Read our reviews on Google** buttons then go straight to your listing. If it's blank, they search Google Maps for the business name and address instead.
- **Map latitude and longitude.** In Google Maps, right-click the office and click the two numbers at the top of the menu to copy them. Paste the first into latitude and the second into longitude. Fill in both or leave both blank.

Save, preview, and publish.

## 9. Give or remove editing access

For security, editing access is managed in Supabase, the service that stores the website's content. This takes about five minutes.

**Add a family member**

1. Ask them to go to https://foxriverrecreation.com/admin and click **Continue with Google** once. They'll see a "not approved" message. That's expected.
2. Sign in to https://supabase.com/dashboard and open the **Website** project.
3. Go to **Authentication** → **Users**, find their email, and copy their **User UID**.
4. Go to **SQL Editor**, paste the following, replace the placeholder with the UID you copied, and click **Run**:

   ```sql
   insert into public.site_owners(user_id) values ('PASTE-THE-USER-UID-HERE');
   ```

5. Ask them to sign in again. The editor will open.

**Remove someone**

In the **SQL Editor**, run:

```sql
delete from public.site_owners where user_id = 'PASTE-THE-USER-UID-HERE';
```

They lose editing, publishing, and upload access right away, even if they're signed in at that moment.

## 10. If something goes wrong

| Message or problem                                                | What to do                                                                                                                     |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| "This Google account isn't approved to edit the site"             | Sign out of Google and sign in with your approved account, or add the account ([section 9](#9-give-or-remove-editing-access)). |
| "Another owner changed this draft"                                | Someone else saved changes while you were editing. Copy your edits somewhere, reload the page, and make them again.            |
| "Your owner session has expired"                                  | Sign in again. Unsaved edits may be lost, so copy long text first.                                                             |
| "Choose a file smaller than…" or "Choose a supported file format" | Check the file type and size limits in [section 4](#4-add-swap-or-remove-photos) or [section 5](#5-change-the-drone-video).    |
| **Publish changes** is greyed out                                 | Click **Save draft** first. If it's still greyed out, the live site already matches your draft.                                |
| The live site doesn't show my change                              | Make sure you clicked **Publish changes** and confirmed. Then refresh the page, or open it in a private window.                |
| A price shows "Please call"                                       | Its price field is blank. Enter a number in the **Rates** tab.                                                                 |

For anything else, such as a new page, a new kind of rate, or a layout change, contact your developer.
